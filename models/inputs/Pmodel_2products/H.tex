Optim problem:
    $\MAX = \int_0^T (\frac{1}{1-\beta} * (a_h * C(t, 1)^{\rho_h} + ((1-a_h)*C(t, 2)^{\rho_h})^{\frac{1}{\rho_h}})^{1-\beta} * \exp{-\delta_h*t})dt$: functional

Actives:
    $C(t, 1)$: consume function of goods 1
    $C(t, 2)$: consume function of goods 1
    $N(t)$: current cash
    $\pi(t)$: profit
    $\frac{dN(t)}{dt} = \pi(t) - p_{1}(t)*C(t,1) - p_{2}(t)*C(t,2)$: financial balance
    $N(T) \geq 0$: cash non-negativeness

External:
    $p_{1}(t)$: price of goods 1
    $p_{2}(t)$: price of goods 2
    $\delta_h$: discount
    $\rho_h$: param

Parameters:
    $T$: Horizon
    $t$: time
    $\beta$: beta
