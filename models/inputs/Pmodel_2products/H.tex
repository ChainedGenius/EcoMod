Optim problem:
    $\MAX = \int_0^T (\frac{1}{1-\beta} * (a_{h} * C(t, 1)^{\rho_{h}} + ((1-a_{h})*C(t, 2)^{\rho_{h}})^{\frac{1}{\rho_{h}}})^{1-\beta} * \exp{-\delta_{h}*t})dt$: functional

Actives:
    $C(t, 1)$: consume function of goods 1
    $C(t, 2)$: consume function of goods 1
    $N(t)$: current cash
    $\pi_{P}(t)$: profit
    $\frac{dN(t)}{dt} = \pi_{P}(t) - p_{1}(t)*C(t,1) - p_{2}(t)*C(t,2)$: financial balance
    $N(T) \geq 0$: cash non-negativeness
    $N(T)$: Terminal value of cash

External:
    $p_{1}(t)$: price of goods 1
    $p_{2}(t)$: price of goods 2
    $\delta_{h}$: discount
    $\rho_{h}$: param
    $a_{h}$: macroparam

Parameters:
    $T$: Horizon
    $t$: time
    $\beta$: beta

Config:
    $T=1$: Horizon value
